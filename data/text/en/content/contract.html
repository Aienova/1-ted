Partnership Contract Terms

1. Purpose of the Contract

The purpose of this contract is to define the partnership conditions between 1-TED and its commercial partners to facilitate the recruitment of qualified talents.

2. Contract Duration

The partnership contract is concluded for an initial period of 12 months, automatically renewable for periods of 12 months, unless terminated by either party with 3 months' notice.

3. Obligations of 1-TED

  • Provide a high-performance and secure platform
  • Ensure technical and commercial support
  • Guarantee data confidentiality
  • Provide tracking and reporting tools

4. Partner Obligations

  • Respect 1-TED's quality charter
  • Provide accurate and up-to-date information
  • Respect candidate confidentiality
  • Pay agreed service fees

5. Pricing

Rates are defined according to the current pricing grid and may vary depending on recruitment volume and subscribed services. Preferential conditions can be negotiated for long-term partnerships.

6. Termination

Either party may terminate the contract at any time with 3 months' notice. In case of serious breach of contractual obligations, termination may be immediate.

For more information about our partnership terms, please do not hesitate to contact us.
