Privacy Policy

1-TED collects and processes your personal data in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The data collected includes: first name, last name, email address, phone number, resume, and other professional information you choose to share.

Your data is used to:

3. Data Sharing

Your data may be shared with partner companies only as part of the recruitment process and with your explicit consent. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights

In accordance with GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access to your data
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to portability
  • Right to object

6. Cookies

Our site uses cookies to improve your user experience. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time in your browser settings.
